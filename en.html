CORAGGIO:

/co-rà-ggio/

…from the Vulgar Latin coraticum, a word built from the root “cor” (meaning heart) and the suffix “-aticum” (which became -aggio in Italian and denotes an action or a state).

It’s the everyday Italian word for cheering someone on. When someone tells you “Coraggio!”, they mean “Chin up!”, “Be strong!”, “Put your heart into it, you’ve got this.”

Coraggio isn’t just courage; at its oldest root it means living with your heart first.
